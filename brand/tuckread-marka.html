The quiet case for reading slowly

essays.example · 9 min read · Saved 2 days ago
SummarySkimming feels efficient but leaves little behind. Slower reading builds memory and understanding. Setting aside one calm block a day is enough to start.

We save more than we read. The list grows on its own, and every unread title feels like a small debt. The fix is not reading faster, it is choosing a calmer time to read. A single quiet half hour can clear more than a week of stolen minutes.
